**Description:**

Stop dreading January.

Freelancer Finance OS is a single spreadsheet that answers the three questions every UK
freelancer loses sleep over:

- **How much have I actually made?** — Log invoices in seconds; paid, awaiting and overdue
  amounts are tracked automatically.
- **How much tax will I owe?** — A built-in estimator for Income Tax *and* Class 4 National
  Insurance, using real HMRC 2025/26 rates (personal allowance taper included). Updates live
  as you log income.
- **How much should I set aside right now?** — A running set-aside figure so the tax bill is
  never a surprise.

**What's inside:**
- 📊 Dashboard — profit, outstanding invoices, tax estimate, monthly breakdown, all automatic
- 🧾 Income Log — 500 rows, invoice status tracking with dropdowns
- 💳 Expense Log — categorised, with allowable-expense tracking
- ⚙️ Tax Settings — every rate and threshold editable, so the sheet never goes out of date
- 📖 Worked examples and a 2-minute setup guide

Works in **Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice**. No macros, no add-ins, no subscription —
buy once, use every tax year (just update the thresholds).

*Note: gives an estimate for sole traders on the cash basis — a planning tool, not tax advice.*

**FAQ section:**
- *Does it work in Google Sheets?* Yes — upload the file to Drive and open with Sheets.
- *I'm not in the UK — will it work?* The logs and dashboard work anywhere; the tax estimator
  is built for UK sole traders (all rates are editable if you want to adapt it).
- *Do I need Excel skills?* No. You only ever type into two log tabs. Everything else is automatic.
