**URL:** https://www.bowlinglaw.co.uk/
**Page titles (verbatim):** "Best Legal Consultant in London UK : bowlinglaw.co.uk", "Best Legal Consultant in London UK : History of the firm", "Best Legal Consultant in London UK : Contact us I Call I Email". **Keyword spam on a regulated law firm,** and a comparative "best" claim that could raise issues with the SRA's rule against misleading clients.

**Phone:** 020 8221 8000 · **Email:** info@bowlinglaw.co.uk

**Services:** residential property, commercial property, corporate, dispute resolution, family and children, employment, banking and finance, healthcare, private client, property litigation, wills, lasting powers of attorney, probate

## Compliance (SRA)
- **SRA Transparency Rules:** publish prices and service information for conveyancing, probate, some employment work and more. The demo has a "Prices" section (placeholders).
- **Show the SRA number and the digital badge,** the complaints procedure and the professional indemnity insurance details. There's a footer placeholder.
- **Remove "Best …" claims.** Unverifiable superlatives are a regulatory risk.

## Why it's a high-value prospect
Law firms bill thousands per matter, and residential conveyancing alone is a volume business that lives on its website. **Pitch the £1,200 Rebrand plus Care.**

1. **"Stratford's solicitors since 1957"**, with the founder's story (the £175 rent is a great detail).
2. **Clean, compliant titles:** fix the spam and the regulatory risk in one go.
3. **Services grouped for people and for businesses.**
4. **A price transparency section,** required and good for conversion.
5. **"Book a consultation"** with the phone number in the header.
