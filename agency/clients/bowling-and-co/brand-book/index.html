01 · Story

Stratford's solicitors since 1957.

On 17 July 1957, Gilbert Bowling began practising in a few rooms above Burton's on Stratford Broadway, having talked the rent down from £200 to £175 a year. Nearly seventy years later, Bowling & Co is still on the Broadway, advising individuals and businesses across East London. The brand should feel like a good solicitor: calm, clear, trustworthy, and human.

Positioning

Stratford's long-established law firm. Clear advice for people and businesses, from a firm that's been on the Broadway since 1957.

02 · Logo

The Seal.

A simple round seal with an italic "B&": the firm's initial and the ampersand of partnership. It's traditional without being stuffy. Copper on slate, like a brass nameplate on a Broadway door. The wordmark is set in EB Garamond.

03 · Colour

Slate, parchment, copper.

Deep slate is authority without the cliché of navy-and-gold. Parchment is warmth, and copper, like a brass nameplate, marks actions and details.

05 · Voice

Plain English, always.

Explain the law the way you would to a friend across the desk. No Latin, no superlatives, and no promises of outcomes (SRA). Be warm on family matters and precise on business ones.

Do

"Buying your first home? Here's exactly what we'll do, and what it costs."

Don't

"Best Legal Consultant in London UK"

Do

"Stratford's solicitors since 1957."

Don't

"London's #1 law firm."

Do

"Going through a separation is hard. We'll take it one step at a time."

Don't

"Aggressive litigators who WIN!"

06 · Imagery

People, not gavels.

Natural portraits of the team at work and in meetings (with consent), the Broadway office front, and Stratford streets. No gavels, scales of justice or handshake stock photography.
