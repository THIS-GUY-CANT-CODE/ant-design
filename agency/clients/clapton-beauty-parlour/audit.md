# Audit: Clapton Beauty Parlour

**URL:** https://www.claptonbeautyparlour.co.uk/
**Industry:** Unisex hair and beauty salon
**Location:** 21 Lower Clapton Road, London E5 0NS
**Phone:** 020 8985 4329
**Hours:** Tue–Fri 10am–6pm · Sat 9am–5pm · Sun and Mon closed
**History:** opened in 1930 by newlyweds Jean and Emanuel Manning. Their daughter Marcia Linch is associated with running it. A 15-year-old Vidal Sassoon (a family relation) used the salon while his own workplace was being repaired after bomb damage. The Hackney Gazette called it the "in shop" for the Beverley Sisters.
**Press:** Spitalfields Life (2016), Hackney Gazette, Hackney Post, East Side Story LDN
**Services:** hairdressing, colour, men's grooming, hair extensions, wedding packages, advanced electrolysis, beauty therapy, manicures, pedicures, facials, spray tanning, sunbeds
**Booking:** listed on Fresha (note: a Fresha listing called "Infinite Hair" also uses 21 Lower Clapton Road, so check whether a second business shares the space)
**Socials:** Facebook, Instagram `claptonbeautyparlour`

## Visible without visiting
- **The homepage title is keyword-stuffed with the phone number:** "Beauty Salon/ unisex Hairdressers Clapton Beauty Parlour 0208 985 4329 | LONDON". It reads as untrustworthy and wastes the best story in Hackney hairdressing.
- **Press coverage exists but isn't used.** Spitalfields Life and the Gazette are free credibility.

## Compliance
- Sunbeds: UK law bans under-18s and the ASA restricts health claims, so the demo lists them in the price list without promotion. Electrolysis is described factually.

## The angle
1. **"Since 1930" plus Art Deco.** The founding year gives a whole visual language no competitor can copy.
2. **The Vidal Sassoon story** as a feature section. It's shareable and press-friendly.
3. **An online booking button** (Fresha) on every screen.
4. **A clear price list** grouped into Hair, Beauty and Body.
5. **A press strip:** Spitalfields Life, Hackney Gazette, Hackney Post.
