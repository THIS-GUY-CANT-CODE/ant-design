A little salon history

The day Vidal Sassoon borrowed the keys.

Newlyweds Jean and Emanuel Manning opened the Clapton Beauty Parlour in 1930. In the 1940s, a 15-year-old Vidal Sassoon, a relative of the family, needed somewhere to work while bomb damage was repaired at his own salon. Emanuel handed him the keys.

The Hackney Gazette remembers the Parlour as the "in shop" for the Beverley Sisters.

"Vidal's aunt was the cousin of my grandmother."Marcia Linch, daughter of the founders, to the Hackney Gazette
