01 · Story

Two roads. One table.

Green Papaya is a family kitchen where Northern Vietnam and Xi'an share one menu. That mix is the brand: generous, fresh and a little unexpected. It's a neighbourhood place that has earned its regulars over twenty years on Mare Street.

Positioning

The only place in Hackney where you can order bún thịt nem nướng and zha jiang noodles in the same round.

Promise

Hanoi meets Xi'an on Mare Street.

Values

Family first.
Fresh every day.
Generous portions, honest prices.
Proudly Hackney.

02 · Logo

The Papaya.

A green papaya cut lengthways, with three seeds down the centre. It's the restaurant's namesake and the base of their signature salad. The wordmark is set in Fraunces Bold, with "Xi'Viet" in spaced caps underneath.

03 · Colour

From the market stall.

Every colour comes from the kitchen: papaya skin, young papaya flesh, rice paper, chilli and turmeric. Leaf green and rice paper do most of the work, and chilli marks the action (book, call, order).

04 · Type

Warm serif, clean sans.

Fraunces gives headlines the feel of a handwritten menu board, with italics for the "meets". DM Sans keeps menus and hours easy to read on a phone. Both are free on Google Fonts.

05 · Voice

Like the owner at your table.

Warm, proud of the food, and short. Name dishes in their own language first, then explain them simply. Never exoticise the food. It's everyday family cooking.

Do

"Bún thịt nem nướng: rice vermicelli, grilled pork, spring rolls, herbs."

Don't

"Dish 42: Pork noodle."

Do

"Come hungry."

Don't

"Embark on an exotic journey to the mystical Orient."

06 · Imagery

Overhead, steam, hands.

Shoot from overhead on the real tables, in natural light from the front window. Show hands reaching in, noodles being lifted and steam rising. Include the family in the kitchen. No stock photos, no chopsticks on a black slate.
