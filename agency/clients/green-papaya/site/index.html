Our story

A Hackney family kitchen.

Green Papaya has been cooking on this corner of Hackney for over twenty years. It started as a Northern Vietnamese kitchen, and over time the family brought in the noodles and street food of Xi'an. The result is a menu you won't find anywhere else in London.

It's still family-run, and everything is still cooked fresh.
