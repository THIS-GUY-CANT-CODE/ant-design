01 · Story

Roman Road's florist since 1950.

Mr Dennington opened his flower shop on Roman Road in 1950. In the 1970s it passed to a new family, and the grandson of the man who took it over still works there today. It's known across the East End for wedding and funeral flowers. The brand should feel graceful and dependable, a florist you trust with the biggest days of your life.

02 · Logo

The Posy.

A four-petal bloom in a round frame, like a posy seen from above or a wreath. It suits both a wedding invitation and a sympathy card, which matters for a florist that does both. The wordmark is set in Prata, an elegant, slightly old-fashioned serif.

03 · Colour

Violet, cream, marigold.

Deep violet has a quiet dignity that suits both celebrations and remembrance. Cream is soft and marigold is warmth, used only on dark backgrounds or as a detail. Lilac mist is for panels.

04 · Type

Graceful serif, friendly sans.

Prata is elegant and warm for headlines and card messages. Mulish is soft and easy to read for prices and ordering details. Both are free on Google Fonts.
