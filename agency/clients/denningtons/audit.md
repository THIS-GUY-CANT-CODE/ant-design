# Audit: Denningtons Florist

**URL:** https://www.denningtonsflowers.co.uk/ (dated `.htm` pages; titles like "Home Page | Denningtons Florist in Bow | Flowers in Bow | London |")
**Industry:** Florist, specialising in weddings and funerals
**Location:** 461 Roman Road, Bow, London E3 5LX
**Phone:** 020 8981 1121 · **Hours:** Mon–Sat 9am–5:30pm, Sun closed (per listings)
**History:** Mr Dennington opened in 1950 a few doors down, and the shop moved to 461 Roman Road about 12 years before the Roman Road LDN article. In the 1970s the business was taken over by Lee Adams's grandfather, and Lee's family has worked there ever since. It has "a loyal following of local East Enders and the odd celebrity".
**Services:** wedding and funeral flowers (specialism), other occasions, local and international delivery
**Press:** Roman Road LDN ("Why we love Denningtons"), Time Out, Tower Hamlets Slice

## ⚠️ Portfolio note
This is our second florist (after Kelly's, Well Street E9). They're 2 miles apart with different specialisms (Denningtons: weddings and funerals, East End heritage). Don't show one the other's concept. Brand them clearly differently.

## The pitch angle
1. **"Roman Road's florist since 1950"**, the East End family story.
2. **Weddings and funerals as the two main paths,** since that's their specialism and their highest-value orders.
3. **International delivery** mentioned explicitly (a relay network?).
4. **Clean titles and Florist schema markup.**
5. **Seasonal banners** on the Care plan.
