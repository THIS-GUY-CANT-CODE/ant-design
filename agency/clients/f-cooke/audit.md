# Audit: F. Cooke, Hoxton

**URL:** none found. Instagram only (`@f.cooke150`), so a "no website" prospect.
**Industry:** Traditional pie, mash and eel shop
**Location:** 150 Hoxton Street, London N1 6SH (London Borough of Hackney)
**Phone:** 020 7729 7718
**Hours (per listings, conflicting, so confirm):** Mon–Thu 10am–7pm · Fri–Sat 9:30am–8pm · Sun closed
**History:** the Cooke family's pie and mash business dates back to 1862 and is described in listings as "London's first pie & mash family". The Cookes are one of the three families most associated with London pie and mash (London Museum). The Hoxton shop is still run as a family business.
**Press:** Time Out, London the Inside ("best pie & mash shops"), Tripadvisor, goop

## Why it's a prospect
- **No website.** Tourists and food writers look for "best pie and mash London", and F. Cooke has no page of its own.
- **Listings contradict each other** (hours, and one even categorises it as a "pop-up restaurant"). An official site with schema markup fixes Google's knowledge panel.
- **Heritage food is a tourism draw.** A simple "what is pie and mash?" explainer converts visitors.

## The pitch angle
1. **Your own website** with the 1862 family story.
2. **"First time? Here's how to order"**: pie, mash and liquor, with eels hot or jellied, explained.
3. **Correct hours and location** everywhere (site plus schema), ending the conflicting listings.
