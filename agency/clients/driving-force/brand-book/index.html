01 · Story

Honest cars, honest work.

Driving Force is a family-run garage on Lea Bridge Road, doing MOTs, servicing, repairs and used car sales for more than 25 years, and trusted enough to join Which? Trusted Traders and the Good Garage Scheme. The brand should say one thing clearly: straight-talking, reliable, quick. No grease-monkey clichés and no hard sell.

Positioning

Walthamstow's family garage: MOTs, servicing and used cars you can trust, from people who'll tell you straight.

02 · Logo

The Forward Chevrons.

Two chevrons pointing forward, like road markings: motion, progress and "you're good to go". The mark works on a van, a key fob, an MOT reminder text or a sign on Lea Bridge Road. The wordmark is set in Barlow Condensed, the look of road signage.

03 · Colour

Road navy, signal yellow.

Deep road-sign navy for trust and signal yellow for action. It's the colour of MOT pass certificates and road markings. Keep yellow for buttons and the chevrons. Tyre black and light grey support.

05 · Voice

Straight talk, no jargon.

Explain what's wrong, what it costs and how long it takes, in plain English. Never pressure. Never scare people about safety to upsell.

Do

"We'll call you before doing any work that isn't booked."

Don't

"Quality Affordable Cheap MOT Used Car Services"

Do

"MOT today? Call 020 8521 1882."

Don't

"Your one-stop automotive solutions partner."

Do

"Family-run on Lea Bridge Road."

Don't

"London's No.1 garage!!!"

06 · Imagery

Clean bays, real people.

The workshop tidy and well lit, the team at work, cars on the ramp, and the forecourt with used stock. Straight-on used-car photos with consistent angles. No stock models in overalls, and no "thumbs up" mechanics.
