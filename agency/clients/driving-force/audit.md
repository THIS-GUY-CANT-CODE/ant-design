# Audit: Driving Force

**URL:** https://drivingforcelondon.com/
**Page title (verbatim):** "Driving Force Quality Affordable Cheap MOT Used Car Services | Used Cars | Walthamstow MOT | London Car Servicing | Quality Affordable Cheap Used cars of Walthamstow | MOT in London | Car Servicing in London". **Extreme keyword stuffing,** which Google ignores or penalises and which reads as spam to people.
**Industry:** MOT testing, servicing and repairs, used car sales
**Location:** 817–823 Lea Bridge Road, Walthamstow, London E17 9DS (the site says 5 minutes from the M11 and A406)
**Phone:** 020 8521 1882 · **Email:** drivingforcelondon@hotmail.com (a **Hotmail address**, which is a trust hit)
**History:** family-run. Listings variously say "over 25 years" and "over 30 years", and one directory says "established 1938". **Confirm before using any date.** The demo uses "25+ years".
**Services:** MOT, used cars, servicing, diagnostics, new tyres, puncture repair, computerised wheel alignment (tracking), air con regas
**Accreditation:** Which? Trusted Traders, Good Garage Scheme. Also on Fixter, WhoCanFixMyCar and MOT Local.
**Reviews:** consistently positive (for example, "MOT completed in 1.5 hours with absolutely great service")

## Why it's a strong prospect
- **The title tag is a textbook SEO mistake.** It's easy to show them the fix, and it has a measurable result.
- **Garages earn repeat revenue** (annual MOTs, servicing). A "book your MOT" flow and reminders are direct income.
- **Used car sales** is a separate high-value funnel that deserves its own page (upsell).

## The pitch angle
1. **Clean title and meta, plus AutoRepair schema.**
2. **Book an MOT or service** above the fold, and tap-to-call.
3. **Which? Trusted Trader and Good Garage Scheme badges** as trust (use official badges only with their permission).
4. **A price list** for MOT, service and air con (the MOT fee maximum is set by DVSA).
5. **Domain email,** included in the setup.
