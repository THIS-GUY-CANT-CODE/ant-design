## The angle
1. **Lead with "Since 1938" and "4.9★ from 684 reviews"**, the two strongest trust signals any locksmith in E2 could have.
2. **Split two jobs clearly:** *I'm locked out* (emergency number, big and red) and *I need a key, remote or paint* (visit the shop).
3. **Show a remote-copying compatibility check.** It's the highest-margin walk-in service and nobody else explains it.
4. **Fix titles and meta, and add LocalBusiness/Locksmith schema.** This is fast SEO for "locksmith Bethnal Green".
5. **Opening hours with a live "open now" status.**
