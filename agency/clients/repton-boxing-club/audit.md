# Audit: Repton Boxing Club

**URLs:** https://www.reptonboxingclub.co.uk/ (titled "Repton Boxing Club - Professional Boxing Gym") **and** https://www.reptonboxingclub.com/ (a separate site). **Two competing official-looking websites.**
**Industry:** Amateur boxing club (community and sport)
**Location:** 116 Cheshire Street, Bethnal Green, London E2 6EG, a former Victorian bath house, the club's home since 1971
**Phone:** 020 7739 8567 (a second number, 020 7739 3595, also appears in listings)
**History:** established 1884 by Repton School to support boys in the East End. Earlier homes include a townhouse in Bethnal Green, Victoria Park Square and Pollard Row. Widely regarded as the oldest amateur boxing club in the UK, it has produced Olympic gold medallists and world title holders (per Wikipedia and press).
**Training (per the club's site, confirm):** Seniors (17–28) Mon/Wed/Fri 4:30–7:30pm · Juniors (11–16) Tue/Thu 4:30–7:30pm · Sunday 10:30am–1:30pm for competition prep · Saturday nursery for 8–10-year-olds. New seniors should come on a Mon/Wed/Fri to see the Senior Coach.
**Press:** Huck ("A portrait of the UK's oldest boxing club"), Tower Hamlets Slice, Layers of London, British Vintage Boxing

## Visible without visiting
- **Two domains (.co.uk and .com) with different content.** People can't tell which is real, and Google splits the authority between them.
- **"Professional Boxing Gym" in the title,** but it's an amateur club, which confuses the audience.
- **A contact email on an unrelated domain** in the listings. That's a trust issue, so check who manages the sites.

## Sensitivities
- **Don't glamorise the Kray twins connection** (they trained here). Lead with the youth work and the champions.
- **Safeguarding:** any photos of junior members need parental consent, and a safeguarding page should be linked.
- **It's a charity or community club, so the budget is limited.** Pitch this as a consolidation job (one site, one domain) plus Care for fixtures and news. A sponsor could fund it.

## The pitch angle
1. **One site, one domain.** Redirect the other domain.
2. **"The UK's oldest amateur boxing club, since 1884"**, with the bath house as the backdrop.
3. **"How to join" by age group,** with the training times in a clear table.
4. **Location hire and filming.** The building is iconic, which makes it a revenue line.
5. **Support and donate.**
