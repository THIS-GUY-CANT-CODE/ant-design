01 · Story

140 years of East End boxing.

Repton was founded in 1884 by Repton School to give East End boys somewhere to go. Since 1971 it has trained in a former Victorian bath house on Cheshire Street, and it's widely regarded as the oldest amateur boxing club in the UK, with Olympic and world champions among its alumni. The brand should feel like the gym: disciplined, proud, no frills, and open to every kid who walks in.

02 · Logo

The 1884 Shield.

A heraldic shield (a nod to the club's Repton School origins) carrying the one number that matters: 1884. It's bold enough for a vest, a ring apron or a gym wall. The wordmark is just "Repton" in Anton, tall and condensed like fight-night posters.

03 · Colour

Black, canvas, corner red.

Gym black and canvas off-white are the ring. Corner red is used sparingly, for action and for the date. No gradients and no chrome: it's an amateur club, not a pay-per-view event.

05 · Voice

Coach's voice: short and direct.

Straight talk, like the coach: what to do, when to turn up, what it takes. Be proud of the history, but never trade on gangland mythology.

06 · Imagery

Grain, sweat, the bath house.

High-contrast black-and-white or muted colour: skipping ropes mid-swing, coaches' hands on pads, the bath house's tiles and skylights, and honours boards. Junior members only with parental consent, and never in a way that identifies them without it.
