Our history

140 years of East End boxing.

Repton was founded in 1884 by Repton School to support boys in the East End. Boxing was on the top floor of a Bethnal Green townhouse, next to chess, whist and bagatelle.

After homes in Victoria Park Square and Pollard Row, the club moved into a former Victorian bath house on Cheshire Street in 1971. It has trained there ever since, producing Olympic gold medallists and world title holders.
