## Visible without visiting
- **The site is product-catalogue led** (`/products/blinds/roman-blinds`, `/products/wood-shutters`). The title leads with a generic keyword and not the 144-year story.
- **The biggest trust asset, "since 1882" and Roman Road's oldest trader, is buried.**

## Why it's a high-value prospect
Interiors jobs are big tickets (shutters, whole-house flooring). One extra measure-and-quote booking a month pays for a Rebrand. **Pitch the £1,200 tier plus Care.**

## The pitch angle
1. **"Dressing East London's homes since 1882"** as the headline.
2. **A "Book a free home measure" CTA,** which is where their revenue starts.
3. **The showroom as a destination:** 470m², two floors, thousands of samples.
4. **Product categories as clear cards,** not a catalogue maze.
5. **A before/after gallery of real installs** (placeholder).
