01 · Story

Dressing East London's homes since 1882.

Abbott's has been trading on Roman Road since 1882, perhaps the oldest surviving trader on one of London's oldest market streets. Today it's a 470m² showroom of blinds, shutters, curtains and flooring. The brand should feel like a beautifully finished room: warm, considered, crafted, and still local.

Roman Road's oldest trader. Made-to-measure blinds, shutters and flooring, with 140 years of know-how.

02 · Logo

The Shutter Window.

A window with two louvred shutter panels: it shows exactly what they make, and reads as a sturdy, honest shopfront mark. Ochre slats bring warmth. The wordmark is "Abbott's" in Playfair Display, a Victorian-era serif for a Victorian-era firm.

03 · Colour

Charcoal, linen, ochre.

Charcoal and linen are the calm neutrals of a well-dressed room. Ochre is warm timber and brass fittings. Sage and oak are supporting accents for product categories. Let the photography of real rooms carry the colour.

05 · Voice

Expert, warm, never pushy.

Talk like the fitter who's measured ten thousand windows: practical advice first, sales second.

Do

"Not sure between shutters and blinds? We'll bring samples and talk you through it."

Don't

"Massive SALE! Blinds from £9.99!!!"

Do

"Dressing East London's homes since 1882."

Don't

"Premium bespoke window solutions provider."

Do

"Come and see 470m² of samples on Roman Road."

Don't

"Browse our extensive online catalogue."

06 · Imagery

Real rooms, real light.

Finished installs in East London homes (with permission), with morning light through shutters and close-ups of fabric, grain and fittings. Show the showroom floors, and the family and fitters at work. Avoid glossy stock interiors that look nothing like a Bow terrace.
