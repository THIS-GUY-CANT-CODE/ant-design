# Audit: Brick Lane Bookshop

**URL:** https://bricklanebookshop.org/ (email address is on a different domain, `info@bricklanebookshop.co.uk`)
**Industry:** Independent bookshop
**Location:** 166 Brick Lane, London E1 6RU
**Phone:** 020 7247 0216 · **Hours:** every day 10am–6pm (per listings)
**History:** traces back to April 1977, when the THAP Bookshop (part of the Tower Hamlets Arts Project) launched in Watney Market. Established 1978, moved to Whitechapel near the London Hospital in 1980, renamed "Eastside" in 1994, and now Brick Lane Bookshop. Readings and launches over the years include Michael Rosen, Adrian Mitchell, Andrea Levy, Rachel Lichtenstein, Hanif Kureishi, Jeanette Winterson, Sarah Waters, James Sallis, Beryl Bainbridge, Lionel Shriver, Jennifer Worth, Sarah Wise and Iain Sinclair.
**Team:** Denise (long-standing), joined in recent years by Kalina and Kate, who built the social media presence.
**Press:** Spread the Word ("London's Bookshops"), East Side Story ("Interest in independence has saved us")

## Visible without visiting
- **Messy URLs** such as `/history/Our%20History%20-%20The%20Bookshop.html` (a file name with spaces), a sign of a hand-assembled site.
- **Two domains** (.org for the site, .co.uk for email), which is confusing.
- **Tourist footfall on Brick Lane is huge.** The site should convert "bookshop near Brick Lane" searches into visits.

## ⚠️ Portfolio note
This is our second bookshop (after Newham Bookshop), so the design is deliberately different: Brick Lane street energy against Newham's community warmth. Don't pitch both with the same email.

## The pitch angle
1. **"Brick Lane's bookshop since 1978"**, with the famous-authors wall.
2. **Open every day, 10–6,** with a visit block tuned for tourists.
3. **An events calendar** (they have a strong reading-events history).
4. **East London and Brick Lane local-interest books** as a signature section.
5. **One domain for both email and site.**
