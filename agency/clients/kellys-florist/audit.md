# Audit: Kelly's Florist

**URL:** https://kellys-florist.ueniweb.com/ (a free UENI template site)
**Industry:** Florist (retail)
**Location:** 217–219 Well Street, Hackney, London E9 6QU
**Phone:** 020 8986 0250
**History:** family-run, trading for over 90 years (per directory listings; ask for the founding year and family story)
**Offer:** fresh flowers, arrangements and bouquets, Christmas flowers, floral tributes (funerals), occasions, Valentine's, unique designs, luxury selection, plants and gifts

## Visible without visiting
- **The site is on a free UENI subdomain** (`kellys-florist.ueniweb.com`), not their own domain. That's weak for trust and for SEO.
- **The page title is "Kelly'S Florist - Best flower services in London"**, with a capitalisation bug and a generic claim.
- **90 years of family history isn't the headline.** Online delivery networks (Interflora-style sites) outrank them for "florist Hackney".
- **Lookalike domains** (floristhackney.co.uk, flowershackney.co.uk and others) are lead-gen/relay sites competing for their customers. Owning their own domain and Google profile matters.

## The angle
1. **"Well Street's florist for over 90 years"**, the real local family shop versus the relay websites.
2. **A funeral tributes section** written with care. It's the highest-value, most time-sensitive order type, and it's done by phone.
3. **Order by phone, prominently.** Add online ordering (Shopify Lite or Stripe links) as an upsell later.
4. **Their own domain, a Google Business Profile link and Florist schema markup.**
5. **Seasonal peaks** (Valentine's, Mother's Day, Christmas) as banners the Care plan swaps in.

## To confirm
Founding year and family story, opening hours, delivery area and charges, price ranges, and whether they do weddings.
- Tribute types (wreaths, sprays, lettered tributes) are standard for funeral florists but aren't confirmed. Check them.
