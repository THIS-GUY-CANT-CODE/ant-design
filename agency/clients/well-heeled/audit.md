# Audit: Well Heeled

**URL:** none found (directory listings only: Yelp, Thomson Local, Love Bethnal Green), so a "no website" prospect
**Industry:** Shoe repair, key cutting, engraving
**Location:** 443 Bethnal Green Road, London E2 9QH
**Phone:** 020 7739 3608
**Hours (per listings, check them):** Mon–Fri 7am–5pm · Sat 8am–4pm
**History (Spitalfields Life, 2016):** run by Ken Hines, then a veteran of 52 years of shoe repair, who had been at this shop for 27 years (so since about 1989). By 2016 there had once been four shoe repairers in Bethnal Green and he was the only one left. His son Paul opened further Well Heeled branches in shopping centres.
**Company:** Well Heeled London Ltd

## ⚠️ Conflict note
**Rose Locksmith & DIY (149 Bethnal Green Road) is also a prospect and also cuts keys.** They're on the same road and partly compete. If one signs, don't show the other's concept to them, and consider not pitching both in the same month. Lead Well Heeled's positioning with *shoe repair*, not keys.

## Why it's a prospect
- **No website.** "Shoe repair Bethnal Green" searches go to directories and chains (Timpson).
- **A heritage craft with a press story** (Spitalfields Life, "East End Cobblers").
- **Multi-branch family business** (Paul's branches), so there's an upsell: one site covering all branches.
