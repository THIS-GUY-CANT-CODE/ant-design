The craft

Fifty years at the bench.

Shoe repair used to be on every high street in the East End. Bethnal Green once had four cobblers. By 2016, Well Heeled was the last one standing. Well Heeled has been on Bethnal Green Road since the late 1980s, run by the Hines family.

"When Ken started there twenty-seven years ago, there were four shoe repairs in Bethnal Green but he is now the only one."Spitalfields Life, "East End Cobblers" (2016). Check the wording against the article before publishing.

Worth knowing

Repair, don't replace.

Heels first

Catch worn heels early and a quick re-heel saves the whole shoe.

Good shoes last

A well-made pair can be resoled more than once, which is cheaper and greener than buying new.
