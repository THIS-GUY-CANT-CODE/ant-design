01 · Story

The last cobbler standing.

Shoe repair used to be on every East End high street. By 2016, Spitalfields Life reported Well Heeled as the only one left in Bethnal Green, run by Ken Hines after more than fifty years at the bench. The brand should feel like the workshop: honest, sturdy and skilled, with no nonsense.

Positioning

The proper independent cobbler on Bethnal Green Road. Real craft at a fair price, not a chain kiosk.

Promise

Bring it in. We'll sort it.

Values

Repair, don't replace.
Done properly.
Fair prices.
Open early, for working people.

02 · Logo

The Stitched Boot.

A work boot in profile, with a dashed welt stitch and a new heel block in saddle tan. That's exactly what they fix. The wordmark is set in Big Shoulders Display, condensed and industrial like old repair-shop signage.

03 · Colour

Leather, tan, apron.

Dark chocolate leather is the base, saddle tan is the new heel, and a work-apron denim blue sets off the craft story. Stitch cream is the background, and dashed stitch lines are a graphic device throughout.

05 · Voice

Short, straight, skilled.

Talk like someone who's fixed a hundred thousand heels: practical and dry, with the odd bit of humour. Lead with the craft.

Do

"Bring it in. We'll sort it."

Don't

"Your one-stop footwear restoration solution."

Do

"Repair, don't replace."

Don't

"Shop our new range of shoe accessories!"

Do

"Open from 7am, so drop them off on the way to work."

Don't

"Flexible customer-centric service hours."

06 · Imagery

Hands, tools, leather.

Close-ups of hands at the finishing machine, rows of heels and blanks, before-and-after of a resoled shoe on the bench, and the shopfront at 7am with the lights on. Grainy and warm, never glossy.
