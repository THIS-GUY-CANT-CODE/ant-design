01 · Story

Made in a biscuit factory. Made to be devoured.

Biscuit Bunker was founded in 2014 in a converted dog biscuit factory. That origin is the brand: warm, a bit cheeky, and serious about craft. We make content in the way a good bakery makes bread, in batches, with care, so people come back for more.

Positioning

The full-service production company with the craft of a big shop and the personality of an independent.

Promise

Good content. Fetched.

Values

Craft over noise.
Warm, never cute.
Straight talking.
Made to be watched.

02 · Logo

The Bone & Record.

A dog biscuit with a REC light at its centre: where we came from and what we do, in one mark. The red dot is the only place red appears in the identity. Treat it as a signal, not a decoration.

05 · Voice

Warm, cheeky, never cute.

One dog or biscuit pun per page at most, and only where it earns a smile. Everywhere else, talk like a producer who respects your time.

Do

"Got a brief? Throw us a bone."

Don't

"Woof woof! We're paw-sitively the best barking-good video pups!"

Do

"Shoot, record or animate, with a crew sized to the job."

Don't

"We leverage synergistic multi-channel video solutions."

Do

Lead with the work. Let the reel talk first.

Don't

Open with a paragraph about how passionate we are.

06 · Imagery

Behind the lens, not stock.

Use real frames from real projects, graded warm. Behind-the-scenes shots with visible kit (slates, monitors, the REC light) are always on-brand. No handshake stock photography, ever. Crop wide (16:9 or 2.39:1) and let the dark UI frame the image like a screen.
