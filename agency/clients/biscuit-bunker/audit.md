## The angle (what the redesign fixes)

1. **The story is buried.** "Founded in a dog biscuit factory" is the most memorable thing about them, and it should be the brand itself, not a line on the About page.
2. **A video company has to lead with video.** The hero should be a showreel, not a paragraph of text.
3. **SEO pages like "Podcast production London" read like keyword pages.** Fold them into one clear services section with anchor links.
4. **No clear next step.** Add one CTA ("Start a project") and a short brief form.
5. **Work should be filterable** by type (commercial, animation, podcast), because that's how buyers shop for a video company.
