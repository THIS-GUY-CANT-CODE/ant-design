## The angle
1. **"Leytonstone's dentist since 1965",** 60 years of continuity. Very few private practices can say that.
2. **Fees on the page.** Private patients shop on price transparency, and the GDC expects fee information.
3. **"New patient" path:** what's included in the first visit, how to book, and parking/transport.
4. **Nervous patients:** a calm, reassuring tone. It's the number one reason people put off the dentist.
5. **Clean up the titles and add Dentist schema markup.**

## Why it's a strong prospect
A private dental patient is worth hundreds of pounds a year. One new patient a month from the site covers a £1,200 Rebrand in weeks.

## Confirm with the practice before pitching
- The nervous-patient approach (longer first visit, stop signal) is standard good practice, but check it matches how they work.
- Written estimates before treatment (normal for private dentistry, but confirm).
- Opening hours, fees, GDC numbers, transport and parking.
