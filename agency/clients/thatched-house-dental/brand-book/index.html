01 · Story

Sixty years on Leytonstone High Road.

The Thatched House Dental Practice has cared for Leytonstone since 1965, and has been led by Harmik Hambarchian since 2000. Most dental brands look like hospitals. This one should feel like being looked after at home: calm, warm and trustworthy, with modern dentistry underneath.

02 · Logo

A tooth under a thatched roof.

The name, drawn literally: a soft straw roof sheltering a tooth. It's warm, a bit storybook, and the opposite of cold clinical dental branding. That's the point for nervous patients. The wordmark is set in Young Serif.

03 · Colour

Deep teal, warm straw.

Deep teal is a calm, clinical-adjacent colour, but warmer than hospital blue. Straw gold is the thatch and gives the brand warmth. Mist and chalk keep everything feeling clean. Gum pink is used sparingly, for children's material only.

04 · Type

A friendly serif and a round sans.

05 · Voice

Reassuring, plain, honest.

Write for someone who's anxious about the dentist. Use short sentences, explain things, and never shame anyone. GDC guidance: no misleading claims, name the dentist with their GDC number, and make fees clear.

Do

"Haven't been in years? You're very welcome here."

Don't

"Neglected teeth? We'll fix your smile!"

Do

"You'll get a written estimate before we start."

Don't

"Affordable pricing, call for details."

Do

"Leytonstone's dentist since 1965."

Don't

"London's best cosmetic dentist."

06 · Imagery

People, not drills.

Real team portraits in soft daylight, the reception and treatment rooms looking calm and tidy, and patients (with consent) smiling naturally. Avoid close-ups of instruments, gloved hands with needles and stock "perfect veneer" smiles.
