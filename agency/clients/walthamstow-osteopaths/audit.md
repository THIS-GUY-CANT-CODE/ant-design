## Visible without visiting
- **Stale copy:** the site says "over 19 years" but the practice opened in 2000. That's more than 25 years now, so the text hasn't been updated since about 2019.
- **Gmail address** as the main contact. That's a trust hit for a healthcare practice. Recommend a domain email (included in the Refresh tier).
- The history is split across separate pages ("The Practice", "The building and Practice history", "Directions"), and the best story is buried.

## Compliance note (health)
Osteopathy advertising is policed by the ASA/CAP. Only describe conditions in line with CAP guidance for osteopaths, with no cure claims. The demo copy sticks to describing treatments and the practice.

4. **Booking by phone or email above the fold,** ready to add an online booking link later.
