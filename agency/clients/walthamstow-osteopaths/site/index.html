Your first visit

Never seen an osteopath? Here's what happens.

You don't need a GP referral. Wear something comfortable, and bring a list of any medication you take.

1

We listen

We ask about your symptoms, history, work and lifestyle.

2

We examine

We look at how you move and gently assess the areas involved.

3

We explain

We tell you what we think is going on and agree a plan together.

4

We treat

Hands-on treatment usually starts in the same session, with advice to take home.

No.72 St Mary Road

A brewery, a music shop, a hairdresser. Then us.

Older E17 residents might remember No.72 as a brewery, a music shop and, most recently, a hairdresser. By the time we found it, it had fallen into disrepair.

Our story

Twenty-five years, one street away from the High Street.

2000

The practice opens

Iain and Stephen start out in a dental centre on Walthamstow High Street.

2002

We find No.72

With a growing patient list, we take on a run-down Victorian shop on St Mary Road.

2003

Doors open

In August we open with a restored shopfront, the original quarry tiling, and two treatment rooms.

2010

Room to grow

A second phase of building work gives us a third room and space for more therapies.
