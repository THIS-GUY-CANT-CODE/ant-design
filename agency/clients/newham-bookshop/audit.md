# Audit: Newham Bookshop

**URL:** https://www.newhambooks.co.uk/ (hand-built `.php` pages such as `about.php`, `contact.php` and `2020/news2020.php`, a sign of a dated site)
**Industry:** Independent bookshop
**Location:** 743–745 Barking Road, London E13 9ER (under 10 minutes' walk from Upton Park station; buses 5, 58, 104, 115, 147, 330, 376)
**Phone:** 020 8552 9993 · **Email:** info@newhambooks.co.uk
**History:** founded March 1978 as part of the Newham Parents' Centre, from an initiative begun five years earlier to tackle low educational achievement in Newham. Manager Vivian Archer joined ten years later.
**Offer:** books for adults and children of all ages, author events and signings, bookstalls across London, extensive work with local schools and community groups. Sells online via Bookshop.org and Hive.
**Press:** Spitalfields Life ("Happy Birthday Newham Bookshop", 2018), ACHUKA ("The Shakespeare & Company of London's East End"), E7 Now & Then, On the Record ("A Bookshop for All"), Radical Booksellers map

## Visible without visiting
- **The news section is filed by year** (`/2020/news2020.php`), so events and news likely go stale.
- **A 48-year community story and glowing press, but no clear route to order or book an author visit.**

## The pitch angle
1. **"East London's independent bookshop since 1978"**, with the press quotes as proof.
2. **Schools and author visits as a clear service with an enquiry route.** That's their B2B revenue.
3. **Events calendar front and centre,** updated monthly on the Care plan.
4. **"Order online" linking to their Bookshop.org and Hive shops,** so they still earn from online sales.
5. **Accessible and fast,** for a community audience on mobile data.

## Pricing note
Community-rooted business: offer the Refresh (£500) plus Care for event updates. Don't push the Rebrand. Its identity is loved as it is.
