Our story

From a parents' centre to East London's bookshop.

1973

An idea

Local parents begin an initiative to tackle low educational achievement in Newham.

1978

The shop opens

In March, Newham Bookshop opens as part of the Newham Parents' Centre.

1988

Vivian arrives

Manager Vivian Archer joins, ten years after the shop opened.

Today

Still independent

Author events, school visits and bookstalls across London.
