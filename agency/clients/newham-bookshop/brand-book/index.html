01 · Story

Born from a parents' centre, 1978.

Newham Bookshop opened in March 1978 as part of the Newham Parents' Centre, a local response to low educational achievement in the borough. Nearly fifty years later it's East London's independent bookshop, known for author events, bookstalls and work with schools. The brand should feel like the shop: welcoming, bright, clever and for everyone.

02 · Logo

The Open Book.

An open book, two pages in two colours: many voices, one shop. It's deliberately simple, so a child can draw it, because half the shop is for children. The wordmark is set in Literata, a typeface designed for long reading.

03 · Colour

Ink blue, marigold, page.

Deep ink blue is serious about books, marigold is the joy of children's reading, and warm page cream is the background. Coral is a small extra accent for events.

04 · Type

A reader's serif, a friendly sans.

Literata was designed for comfortable long-form reading and gives headlines a literary warmth. Source Sans 3 is clear and friendly for listings and events. Both are free on Google Fonts.

05 · Voice

Warm, bookish, open.

Like a great bookseller: enthusiastic, knowledgeable and never snobbish. Recommend, don't lecture.

Do

"Tell us what you loved last, and we'll find what's next."

Don't

"Curated literary selections for the discerning reader."

Do

"A bookshop for all."

Don't

"Your destination for bestselling titles."

Do

"Author coming to your school? We'll bring the books."

Don't

"Contact our B2B education sales team."

06 · Imagery

Readers, shelves, events.

Children cross-legged in the kids' section (with parental consent), authors signing at events, the bookstall set up at a school, and the Barking Road shopfront. Warm, candid and diverse, because that's who Newham is.
