# Audit: The Queen's Head, Limehouse

**URL:** none found (only Tripadvisor listings and Facebook posts), which makes this a "no website" prospect
**Industry:** Pub, community-run free house
**Location:** 8 Flamborough Street, Stepney/Limehouse, London E14 7LS
**Phone:** listings disagree (one shows 07707 304259), so check before using
**History:** Grade II listed, opened 1827. Believed to have inspired the Queen Vic in EastEnders. The Queen Mother famously pulled a pint here. Reopened in December 2023 by a group of locals as a free house with prices kept at community rates. A "Save Your Queens Head" campaign name appears on Tripadvisor.
**Offer:** premium lagers, local craft beers, food, and quiz nights with mini games
**Press:** Poplar London ("reopens with big goals"), CAMRA/WhatPub, Tripadvisor reviews

## Why it's a prospect
- **No website at all.** People searching "pub Limehouse" or "Queen Vic real pub" land on Tripadvisor, and the story isn't being told anywhere the pub controls.
- **A community-run pub needs regulars and events,** so a what's-on page, quiz nights and private hire are direct revenue.
- **The story is PR gold:** the Queen Mother's pint and the Queen Vic inspiration.

## Pricing note
A community pub may be cash-tight. Offer the £500 Refresh with the £49/mo Care plan covering the weekly what's-on updates. The Care plan is the real value here.
