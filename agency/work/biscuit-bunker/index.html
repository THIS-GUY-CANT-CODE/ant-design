01

What it was

The problems holding the old site back.

  1. The best story they have, that the studio started in a converted dog biscuit factory, sits on the About page instead of being the brand.
  2. Services are spread across separate keyword pages like "Podcast production London", which read like SEO filler.
  3. Search listings show a text-first site. For a video company, the work itself should be the first thing you see.

03

The brand pitch

Good content. Fetched.

The name was already brilliant. We just stopped hiding it. The logo is a dog bone with a red REC dot in the middle, so the factory story and the camera share one mark. Bunker black and biscuit gold do the heavy lifting, with a single hot red saved for anything that means record, play or act now.

  • A dog-bone mark with a REC dot: the story and the craft in one symbol
  • Archivo at its heaviest weight for headlines that read like title cards, and JetBrains Mono for timecode and technical detail
  • Voice: confident, a bit cheeky, and never corporate. "Got a brief? Throw us a bone."
  • A rule for every end card: the bone ident, then "Good content. Fetched."

04

The marketing pitch

Make the studio's own channels the showreel.

Week 1

One case study per project

Turn every Vimeo project into its own page: the brief, the idea and the result. That ranks for "brand film for [sector]" searches in a way thin keyword pages rarely do.

Month 1

"From the Bunker" on LinkedIn

A 60-second behind-the-scenes edit every month, cut in the new brand style. Agency producers hire people they've watched work.

Month 2

The biscuit tin mailer

A tin of dog biscuits sent to 30 target agency producers, with a QR code to the showreel on the lid. It's memorable, cheap and on brand.

Ongoing

Podcast clip engine

Every podcast they produce gives three vertical clips for the client, which is also a free advert for Bunker's podcast service.

Local

Google Business Profile

Claim "video production Shoreditch" with the showreel as the cover video and a project photo every week.

What we'd recommend

Rebrand

Creative agencies judge suppliers on taste. A full identity plus a multi-page site with case studies is the version that wins pitches.
