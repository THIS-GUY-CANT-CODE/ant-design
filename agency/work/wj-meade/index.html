03

The brand pitch

The independent. Since 1953.

A single line forms both a roofline and the M of Meade. Deep navy and London-stock gold give it the weight of a private bank, and a brick red is kept for anything that asks for action, like Book a valuation or SOLD. DM Serif Display gives it quiet authority next to the chains' shouty sans-serifs.

  • A roofline M that works on a sold board, a lanyard or an app icon
  • Brick red for actions only: valuation, SOLD, LET
  • Voice: knowledgeable and neighbourly. "Someone who actually knows your street"
  • A sold-board redesign the whole of E3 will see

04

The marketing pitch

One extra valuation a month pays for all of this many times over.

Week 1

A valuation page per office

Bow, Stratford, Wood Green, Highams Park and Enfield each get their own "What's my home worth in [area]?" page. That's five local search entry points instead of one.

Month 1

New boards and windows

The new identity on every For Sale and To Let board. Boards are an estate agent's biggest free media.

Month 2

"70 years of your street"

An Instagram and email series pairing archive photos with the same street today. Landlords and long-time owners share it, and they're the future instructions.

Quarterly

Area market notes

A short, honest market update per branch area, sent to past clients. It keeps W J Meade top of mind for when they decide to sell.

2028

75 years

A diamond-anniversary campaign across all five offices, with a free valuation event in each.

What we'd recommend

Rebrand + Care across 5 branches

It's the highest-value prospect in the portfolio. Commission on a single sale covers the full rebrand, and five branches means five times the local search footprint.
