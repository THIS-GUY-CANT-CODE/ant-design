What it was

The problems holding the old site back.

  1. The homepage title is keyword-stuffed with the phone number: "Beauty Salon/ unisex Hairdressers Clapton Beauty Parlour 0208 985 4329 | LONDON".
  2. A salon open since 1930, where a teenage Vidal Sassoon once worked, and none of that shows in how it appears on Google.
  3. Coverage in Spitalfields Life and the Hackney Gazette is free credibility that goes unused.

03

The brand pitch

Doing Clapton's hair since 1930.

They opened in 1930, so the brand goes back to its own decade. The logo is an Art Deco sunburst rising over a half-circle, like a salon mirror. Black lacquer, blush and gold make a palette that looks like a powder compact. Bodoni Moda italic brings the glamour, and Jost keeps prices and hours clean.

  • A sunburst mark from the year they opened
  • Lacquer, blush and gold: a palette that belongs on a salon window
  • Voice: glamorous but warm and local. "Pull up a chair."
  • Press credits (Spitalfields Life, Hackney Gazette) always on show

04

The marketing pitch

The countdown to 100 years starts now.

Week 1

Fix the title and the booking path

"Clapton Beauty Parlour: Hair & Beauty, Lower Clapton Road since 1930", with Fresha booking on every page and in the Instagram bio.

Month 1

The story, pitched properly

The Vidal Sassoon and Beverley Sisters history is a gift for local press and heritage accounts. Pitch it with the new site as the link.

Month 1

Deco Instagram templates

A branded grid for before-and-after hair and new colour work, so every post looks like part of the same salon.

Seasonal

Wedding season landing page

Their wedding packages get their own page and a spring campaign aimed at Hackney couples.

2030

The centenary

Clapton Beauty Parlour turns 100 in 2030. A "Countdown to 100" series, with one decade of hairstyles a month, builds to the biggest local story they'll ever have.

What we'd recommend

Rebrand

The story is the asset. An identity worth putting on a window, a booking-first site and templates they can reuse all the way to the centenary.
