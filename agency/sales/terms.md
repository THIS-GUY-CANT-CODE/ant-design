# Second Coat: simple terms of work

*Plain-English terms for small-business website projects. Have a solicitor review these before you rely on them. This is a starting template, not legal advice.*

1. **Previews.** Unsolicited previews are free and carry no obligation. Until you pay, the design stays ours and may appear in our portfolio labelled as a concept. We'll remove it within 7 days if you ask.
2. **Price.** The fixed price in your proposal covers the scope listed there. Extra work is quoted before we start.
3. **Payment.** As set out in the proposal. We put the site live once payment is received.
4. **Ownership.** Once you've paid in full, you own the website files, the brand assets we made for you, and your content. We keep the right to show the project in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
5. **Your content.** You confirm you have the rights to anything you give us (photos, logos, text) and that factual claims about your business are accurate.
6. **Third-party costs.** Domains, paid fonts, stock photos and booking or payment platforms are billed at cost, or paid by you directly.
7. **Revisions.** The number of edit rounds is in your proposal. A "round" is one consolidated list of changes.
8. **Care plan.** Monthly, rolling, 30 days' notice to cancel. If you cancel, we hand over the site files and help you move hosting.
9. **Liability.** We take care, but we're not liable for lost profits or for downtime caused by third-party hosts or platforms. Our total liability is limited to the amount you've paid us.
10. **Data.** Where the site collects enquiries, you're the data controller. We'll set up a basic privacy notice for you to approve.
11. **Law.** These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
