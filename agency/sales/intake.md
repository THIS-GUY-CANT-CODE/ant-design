# Client intake: send this the moment they say yes

Copy the questions into a Google Form or Tally form (or just an email). Each answer maps to a field, shown in brackets, so filling in the concept's content in `web/apps/studio` is copy and paste.

## 1. The basics
1. Business name exactly as you want it shown.
2. Phone number customers should call.
3. Email address for enquiries. We can set up a free `you@yourbusiness.co.uk` address.
4. Full address, and anything that helps people find you ("opposite the Young Prince pub").
5. Opening hours for each day, including closed days.
6. Your domain name, if you have one, and who it's registered with. Or shall we buy one for you?

## 2. What you sell
7. Your main services or products, most important first.
8. Prices, or "from" prices, for your top 5–10 items.
9. Anything you **don't** want to advertise (services you're winding down, and so on).

## 3. Your story
10. When did the business start, and who started it? *(timeline)*
11. Any family history, previous premises or milestones? *(timeline)*
12. Any press coverage, awards or memberships we can mention? We need the name of the award and the year. *(stats, quote)*
13. Two or three customer reviews you're proud of, with the reviewer's first name, or "Google review". *(quote)*

## 4. Photos
14. 5–15 photos: the shopfront, the team, your work and the inside. Phone photos are fine.
15. Anyone in the photos who **shouldn't** be shown (for example, staff who prefer not to be)?
16. For children, patients or clients in photos: do you have their permission to show them online?

## 5. Rules for your trade (we check these, but tell us anything we should know)
- **Dentists:** GDC numbers for everyone named, and your fee list.
- **Solicitors:** SRA number, complaints procedure, and prices for the services covered by the Transparency Rules.
- **Health and therapy:** what you're registered with (GOsC, HCPC and so on).
- **Estate agents:** redress scheme and client money protection membership.
- **Salons with sunbeds:** confirm the under-18 policy.

## 6. Sign-off
17. Who approves changes, and their mobile number?
18. When do you want it live? (Our standard is 7 days from receiving these answers.)
