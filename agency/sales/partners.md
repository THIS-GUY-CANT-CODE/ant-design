# Partnerships, grants and channels

Cold pitching one shop at a time works, but these channels put us in front of dozens of the right businesses at once. **Work through them in this order.**

## 1. Grants: make it "free" for the client

A £500 site is an easy yes if a grant covers most of it.

| Scheme | What it offers | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tower Hamlets × Enterprise Nation: Business Adaptation Project** | Business support plus **up to £1,250 match funding** for 215 small businesses in Tower Hamlets | **Check it's still open, and check the eligibility, before mentioning it** |
| Website/SEO grants via the borough (reported by other local web designers as "£1,000 towards your website") | Up to about £1,000 | Find the actual scheme on the Tower Hamlets business pages and confirm it |
| East End Community Foundation (EECF) | Business grants up to £2,000 for young residents' businesses trading under 2.5 years | Narrow eligibility |
| Foundation for Future London | Community, creative and innovation projects (Hackney, Newham, Tower Hamlets, Waltham Forest) | Project funding, not websites. Relevant for Repton and Newham Bookshop |

**How to use them:** never promise a grant. Say: *"Some Tower Hamlets businesses can get match funding for this. I'll help you check if you qualify."* Then help with the application. That's a service competitors don't offer.

**Rose Locksmith, Well Heeled, Brick Lane Bookshop, Abbott's, Denningtons, Repton and the Queen's Head are all in Tower Hamlets,** so check the scheme before pitching them.

## 2. East End Trades Guild (about 300 independent members)

The Guild is an alliance of about 300 small independent East End businesses (founding organiser: Krissie Nicolson), campaigning on fair rents, rates and "East End Independents' Day". Several of our concepts are exactly its kind of member.

**The offer (keep it generous; it's a relationship, not a sale):**
- **A free "website health check" evening** for members: 10 minutes per business, a printed one-page report from `find-prospects.js`' audit, and practical fixes they can make themselves.
- **A member rate:** £450 Refresh (10% off) and the first month of Care free.
- **Give back:** sponsor or volunteer for Independents' Day with a free "shop local" landing page listing participating members.

**Outreach email to the Guild:**
> Subject: A free website health check for East End Trades Guild members
>
> Hi Krissie,
>
> I run Second Coat, a small East London web studio. I've been redesigning sites for independents like Rose Locksmith (since 1938) and Abbott's on Roman Road (since 1882), as concepts to show what's possible.
>
> I'd love to offer Guild members a free website health check evening: ten minutes each, a one-page report, and fixes they can make themselves at no cost. If anyone wants more, members get a discount. No hard sell.
>
> Could we find a date?
>
> <your name> · Second Coat · <phone>

## 3. Local press: Roman Road LDN / Tower Hamlets Slice, Spitalfields Life, Hackney Gazette

These outlets profiled most of the businesses in our portfolio. They love local-heritage stories, and "a local studio is giving old East End shops a digital makeover" is one.
- **Pitch a story** once 2–3 concepts become real clients (with the owners' permission): before/after, the owner's quote, and the history.
- **Never pitch unsolicited concepts to press.** Only paying or consenting clients.

## 4. Accountants and bookkeepers (referral partners)

Every small-business accountant has clients with bad websites.
- **Offer:** £100 per referred client who signs, or a 10% discount for their client (their choice).
- **Where:** independent accountants in E1–E17. Use `find-prospects.js "accountant"` to list them, and their own weak sites are a pitch too.
- **Pitch line:** *"I make your clients look as good online as they are in person. You get £100 per referral, and they get a better business."*

## 5. Business Improvement Districts and town-centre teams

Roman Road, Hackney Wick, Walthamstow and Stratford all have town-centre or BID teams with small-business programmes. Offer a **street-wide deal**: 5 businesses on the same street get the Refresh at £400 each, plus a joint "shop the street" page.

## 6. Existing clients (the best channel once it exists)
- A referral reward of £100 off or a free month of Care for each signed referral (already in `PLAN.md`).
- Ask every live client for **one intro** to a neighbour, at handover.
