# Outreach

## Email 1 — the reveal (send same day demo is live)
Subject: I rebuilt the <Business> website (no strings)

> Hi <first name or team>,
>
> I run Second Coat, a small web studio in East London. I liked <specific true thing about them> but your site was letting you down on mobile — <top problem from audit, one line>.
>
> So I rebuilt it: <preview link>
>
> It's yours for £500 if you want it live on your domain this week — hosting set up, one round of tweaks included. If not, no hard feelings; the link comes down in 14 days.
>
> <Your name> · Second Coat · <phone>
> Not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

## Follow-ups
- Day 3: one line + a 60s screen recording walking through before/after.
- Day 7: walk in or phone. Being local is the unfair advantage, so use it. Bring the concept on your phone (`/concepts/<slug>`) and show the case study (`/work/<slug>`) on the spot.
- Day 14: "Taking the preview down Friday — want me to keep it up?" Then stop.

## Closing
- Stripe payment link for £500 (Refresh) or £1,200 (Rebrand). Send `sales/intake.md` the same day. Upsell the £49/mo care plan at handover, not before.
- Ask for permission to show them as a client + a one-line testimonial the day they go live.
- Update `pipeline.csv` status: `demo_sent → replied → paid → live → care`.

## Rules
- Only email generic business addresses; always include opt-out + company details (PECR).
- Never pretend the demo is their official site or that they are already a client.
