# Learnings

One line per build. What was slow, and what to turn into a template next time.

- 2026-09-27 · biscuit-bunker · The build sandbox can't reach client sites, so get before screenshots from a real browser. The one-file site plus one-file brand book format works well; next time extract the shared CSS (banner, buttons, grid) into `templates/`.
- 2026-09-27 · green-papaya · Listings disagreed on the address (Mare St vs Kingsland Rd), so always confirm address and hours before pitching. Added `scripts/shot.js <slug>` for after screenshots and an overflow check. Restaurants need a sticky mobile Call/Menu bar and Restaurant schema markup by default.
- 2026-09-27 · rose-locksmith · Trades search results are full of keyword-spam lead-gen domains. Search for old family shops instead ("since 19xx" on a named high street). Added `scripts/brandbook.js <slug>`, which builds the brand book from `brand.json`, so no more hand-written brand books. Grid gotcha: use `minmax(0,1fr)` for grids of fixed-ratio items or phones overflow.
- 2026-09-27 · walthamstow-osteopaths · Stale "over N years" copy is a great pitch hook, because it proves nobody maintains the site. Health businesses need an ASA/CAP check: describe treatments, never promise outcomes. Most demo time now goes on finding verifiable facts, so research first and build second.
- 2026-09-27 · wj-meade · Estate agents are the best-value prospects: high revenue per client, multiple branches (upsell), stale award badges. Lead with the valuation form and pitch the £1,200 tier.
- 2026-09-27 · clapton-beauty-parlour · Heritage press (Spitalfields Life, local Gazette) is the fastest way to find businesses with a real story. Check every historical claim wording against the source ("used the salon" is not "trained here").
- 2026-09-27 · thatched-house-dental · Dentists: GDC rules mean fees and GDC numbers go on the page. Nested-selector gotcha: use `.band>div`, not `.band div`, when inner divs exist.
- 2026-09-27 · queens-head-limehouse · "No website at all" is its own prospect type: search for heritage or community businesses that only have Tripadvisor or Facebook. Portfolio now handles a missing url. Pitch the Care plan (weekly what's-on updates) as the main value.
- 2026-09-27 · kellys-florist · Free-subdomain sites (ueniweb, wixsite, business.site) are instant prospects, so search for them directly. "90 years trading" is not the same as "90 years at this address": word the headline to match the source.
- 2026-09-27 · well-heeled · Watch for prospects competing with each other (Rose and Well Heeled both cut keys on Bethnal Green Road). Flag it in the audit and stagger the pitches. Quotes from articles must be copied from the source, not rebuilt from a search snippet.
- 2026-09-27 · newham-bookshop, repton-boxing-club · The new `scripts/site.js` generator turns a site into a ~80-line JSON brief, about 5× faster than hand-writing HTML. Generator bugs caught on Repton (status pill with no data, hours title, SVG text overflow) are now fixed for every future site.
- 2026-09-27 · driving-force · Search for keyword-stuffed titles ("Quality Affordable Cheap …"). They're the easiest pitch to prove. Directories disagree on founding dates, so use the lowest verified figure and flag the rest.
- 2026-09-27 · brick-lane-bookshop · Generator contrast bugs: a dark accent gave black-on-black buttons, and a light accent gave invisible text on cream. `site.js` now picks button text by accent brightness and falls back to primary for accent-coloured text on light backgrounds. Look at every generated site, even when the checks pass.
- 2026-09-27 · all · Added an automatic contrast check to `shot.js`. It caught 6 hand-built sites with unreadable gold or yellow text on cream. Rule: light accents (gold, yellow, straw) are for dark backgrounds only. On light backgrounds, use a darkened version.
- 2026-09-27 · machine · Added `scripts/find-prospects.js` (Google Places plus an automatic site audit), tested against synthetic good and bad pages. This replaces the slowest manual step, hand-searching for prospects. Run it locally, weekly, per trade.
- 2026-09-28 · the original six · Rebuilt as flagship case studies, each with a signature interaction taken from the business's own story (see revamp skill §3a). A generated site is a baseline, not a portfolio piece. `shot.js` now captures with reduced motion, so scroll-pinned sections don't leave empty gaps in full-page screenshots, and every site has to have a clean static fallback.
- 2026-09-28 · case studies · First drafts of the "what it was" lists mixed real observations with guesses about sites we'd never been able to load. They're now cut back to observed facts only, and each page says where the observations came from. Honesty is the pitch: a business owner will spot a false claim about their own site instantly.
- 2026-09-28 · motion kit · Shared micro-interactions now live in `kit/` and are inlined by `scripts/kit.js`. One mistake cost a round: new site CSS was inserted before the first reduced-motion block, which was inside the kit `<style>`, so the next kit run silently deleted it. Fix: always insert into the site's own `<style>`, and after every build grep for one selector from each new feature.
- 2026-09-28 · everything · **Reset.** The owner rejected the first round as outdated. They were right: heritage palettes (navy and gold), period pastiche (Deco sunbursts, synthwave, sepia, film leaders, bulbs), clipart scenes and old serifs dressed old businesses in old costumes. The whole thing was rebuilt in Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind v4, GSAP, Motion and R3F in `web/`, with one contemporary brand per business: one idea, one typeface family, a small palette with one owning colour, and one signature interaction. The design standard and banned list are now at the top of the revamp skill. Test every new concept against it before showing anyone.
- 2026-09-28 · stack · Gotchas hit: Tailwind v4 doesn't scan workspace packages (add `@source`), React's hooks lint forbids setState in effects (use `useSyncExternalStore`), Next 16 route types need `next typegen` before `tsc`, the parent repo's `.gitignore` hid `pnpm-lock.yaml`, and Playwright must match the preinstalled browsers (1.56.1).
- Motion kit (@sc/ui/motion): `MotionKit` (intro curtain, scroll progress, back to top) goes in every layout; `RollText`, `Tilt`, `Stagger`, `Scramble`, `HoverLetters`, `Skew`, `Parallax`, `ClipReveal`, `Spotlight` cover most micro-interactions. Two traps: per-letter inline-blocks let lines break mid-word (wrap each word in `whitespace-nowrap`), and `HoverLetters` on an accent background needs `[--accent:...]` overridden or the letters vanish on hover.
- Copy: no em dashes anywhere on the sites. Use full stops, commas, colons or "to" for ranges (10am to 6pm).
