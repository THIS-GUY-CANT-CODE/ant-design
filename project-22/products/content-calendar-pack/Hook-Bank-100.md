# The 100-Hook Bank

Fill-in-the-blank opening lines that stop the scroll. Grouped by the job they do.
Swap [brackets] for your niche. Pairs with the Content Calendar spreadsheet.

## Curiosity gaps (1–15)
1. Nobody tells you this about [topic] until it's too late.
2. I did [thing] every day for 30 days. The result made no sense.
3. The [industry] doesn't want this to be common knowledge.
4. There's a number that decides [outcome] — and you've never checked yours.
5. Everything you've been told about [topic] is half right. The half matters.
6. I found the exact moment most people give up on [goal].
7. [Familiar thing] has a hidden setting that changes everything.
8. The most expensive mistake in [niche] costs nothing to avoid.
9. Why does nobody talk about what happens AFTER [milestone]?
10. I asked 50 [professionals] the same question. 48 gave the same wrong answer.
11. This took me [X years] to learn and 30 seconds to explain.
12. The difference between [good result] and [great result] is one boring habit.
13. You're probably in the 90% who do [common thing] backwards.
14. What I know now that I'd tattoo on my forearm before starting [journey].
15. The question I should have asked before spending [money/time] on [thing].

20. [Big %] of [group] fail at [task] — for a reason that takes 2 minutes to fix.
21. The 80/20 of [skill]: two things that carry everything else.
22. Day 1 vs Day 100 of [habit]. The gap is not what you think.
23. 5 minutes of [activity] beats 2 hours of [other activity]. The maths:
24. One [item] costs £[X]. Over ten years, that's a [big thing].
25. 3 signs you're about to [common failure] (I ignored all 3).
26. I read [N] books on [topic] so you don't have to. The 5 ideas that survived:
27. [Tiny number]% of people do this. They get [outsized result].
28. My [metric] went from [low] to [high] in [time]. The boring truth of how:
29. There are only 4 types of [thing]. Everything else is a mix.
30. £1,000. Most people would waste it. Here's the checklist that stops you.

log it in the Content Calendar. Repeat. 100 hooks × any platform = you never start from blank.*
