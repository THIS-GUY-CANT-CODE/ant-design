# The House Move Master Checklist — 8 Weeks to Moving Day (UK)

Pairs with the Moving Budget spreadsheet. Work top to bottom; dates in [brackets] are
relative to moving day (M).

## M–8 weeks — Decide & book
- [ ] Confirm moving date in writing with all parties (solicitor / landlord / agent)
- [ ] Get 3 removals quotes (or price a van hire + mates) → enter winner in Moving Budget
- [ ] If renting: give notice per your tenancy agreement — check the exact notice period
- [ ] Book time off work for moving day + the day after
- [ ] Start the "use it up" freezer campaign — stop buying frozen food now
- [ ] One room per weekend rule starts: declutter room 1 (sell on Vinted/eBay — it funds the move)

## M–6 weeks — Paperwork
- [ ] School transfers / childcare notice if applicable
- [ ] Collect quotes for end-of-tenancy cleaning (renters — it's deposit-critical)
- [ ] Book storage if there's a gap between homes
- [ ] Gather boxes free from supermarkets/Facebook before buying any
- [ ] Photograph valuables and check contents insurance covers the move

## M–4 weeks — Tell people (the big list)
- [ ] Royal Mail redirection — set it up now to start on moving day (takes ~5 working days to activate)
- [ ] Bank(s), employer/payroll, HMRC, DVLA (licence AND vehicle log book), electoral roll
- [ ] GP & dentist (register near the new place — waiting lists are real)
- [ ] Utilities at new home: set up energy, water, broadband (broadband lead times are 2+ weeks — book the engineer NOW)
- [ ] Insurance: buildings/contents for the new address from day one
- [ ] Subscriptions & deliveries: update addresses (your Subscription Audit Tracker has the full list 😉)

## M–2 weeks — Pack for real
- [ ] Pack everything you won't touch in 2 weeks; label boxes by ROOM + one-line contents
- [ ] Number every box; keep a count (the spreadsheet's Misc row covers tape/marker resupply)
- [ ] Arrange pet/child care for moving day
- [ ] Confirm removals booking, parking arrangements and any lift bookings at both ends
- [ ] Run down the fridge; plan last-week meals

## M–1 week
- [ ] Pack the ESSENTIALS BOX (see below) — it travels with you, not in the van
- [ ] Defrost freezer 48h before the move
- [ ] Charge power banks; download offline maps to the new place
- [ ] Withdraw a little cash for moving-day tips/emergencies
- [ ] Meter photos checklist ready for both properties

## Moving day
- [ ] Photograph meters (gas, electric, water) at BOTH properties — timestamped
- [ ] Photograph the empty old place (renters: every room, inside appliances) for the deposit
- [ ] Leave keys as agreed; keep a written note of who got what
- [ ] At the new place: beds built FIRST, kettle on second, everything else is tomorrow's problem

## The Essentials Box (the difference between a hard day and a terrible one)
Kettle, mugs, tea/coffee, milk (UHT), snacks · toilet roll ×2 · hand soap · bin bags ·
phone chargers · basic tools + scissors + tape · lightbulbs · first-aid kit + paracetamol ·
bedding for night one · towels ×2 · change of clothes · important documents folder ·
kids'/pets' comfort items · cleaning spray + cloths

## First week in the new place
- [ ] Submit meter readings to your suppliers (both addresses)
- [ ] Register on the electoral roll & with the council (council tax)
- [ ] Test smoke alarms; find the stopcock and fuse box BEFORE you need them
- [ ] Renters: review the inventory carefully and dispute inaccuracies in writing within the window
- [ ] Update the Moving Budget's Actual column — future-you (and your deposit) will thank you
