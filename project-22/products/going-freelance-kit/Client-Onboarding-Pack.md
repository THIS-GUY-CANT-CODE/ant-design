# The Freelance Client Onboarding Pack

Everything you send between "yes, let's work together" and day one — so you look like an
agency of one, set boundaries early, and never chase basics again.

**Contents:** 5-email welcome sequence · client questionnaire · kickoff checklist ·
boundary-setting scripts. Replace anything in [brackets].

---

## Part 1 — The Welcome Sequence (5 emails)

### Email 1 · The confirmation (send within 2 hours of the yes)

> **Subject: Brilliant — here's what happens next**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Great news — I'm delighted to be working with you on [project].
>
> So you're never wondering where things stand, here's the plan:
>
> 1. **Today:** You'll receive my client questionnaire (10 minutes, promise).
> 2. **[Date]:** I'll send the proposal/contract and first invoice for the deposit.
> 3. **Once both are back:** we book the kickoff call and I start [date].
>
> Nothing is needed from you right now. Speak soon,
> [You]

### Email 2 · The questionnaire (same day)

> **Subject: 10 minutes that will save us both weeks**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Before I start, I want to make sure I'm building the right thing. Could you fill in the
> attached questionnaire by [date]? Short answers are fine — bullet points welcome.
>
> If any question doesn't apply, skip it. If you'd rather talk it through, grab 20 minutes
> here instead: [booking link]
>
> [You]

### Email 3 · Contract + deposit (after questionnaire returns)

> **Subject: Contract + first invoice for [project]**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Two attachments:
>
> 1. **The agreement** — scope, timeline, and what counts as "done". Flag anything that
>    doesn't look right before signing; it's easier to fix now.
> 2. **Deposit invoice** — [X]% to secure your slot, due [date]. Work starts once it lands.
>
> Payment details are on the invoice. Any questions, just reply.
>
> [You]

### Email 4 · Kickoff confirmation

> **Subject: We're on — kickoff [date]**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Deposit received and contract signed — thank you! We're officially on.
>
> Kickoff call: **[date/time]** ([calendar link]). Agenda: 1) confirm goals, 2) agree the
> communication rhythm, 3) what I need from you in week one.
>
> Between now and then, could you send: [asset list — logins, brand files, copy docs].
>
> [You]

### Email 5 · Day-one status (start of work)

> **Subject: [Project] — underway 🚀**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick note to say work has started. You'll get updates every [Friday], and the first
> milestone ([deliverable]) lands [date].
>
> How to reach me: email anytime (replies within [1 business day]). For anything urgent:
> [channel].
>
> [You]

## Part 2 — The Client Questionnaire

**About the project**
1. In one sentence: what does success look like 3 months after this project ships?
2. What prompted this project *now*?
3. What happens if it doesn't get done?
4. Has anyone attempted this before (internally or another freelancer)? What happened?

**About the audience**
5. Who exactly is this for? Describe one real customer/user.
6. What do they currently do instead?

**About taste**
7. Three examples you love (competitors or not) — and one you hate. Why?
8. Any absolute no-gos (colours, words, styles, tech)?

**About logistics**
9. Who gives final sign-off? (One name, ideally.)
10. Hard deadline, or "when it's right"? If hard — what's driving it?
11. Budget ceiling if scope grows: is there flexibility, or is [quoted amount] the line?
12. Everything I'll need access to: [list — they tick what exists]

## Part 3 — Kickoff Checklist (yours, not the client's)

- [ ] Signed contract stored (dated copy)
- [ ] Deposit received and reconciled
- [ ] Questionnaire answers reviewed; open questions listed for kickoff
- [ ] All access/assets received and tested (logins actually work)
- [ ] Project folder created (files, brief, contract, invoices in one place)
- [ ] Kickoff call booked; agenda sent the day before
- [ ] Update rhythm agreed (day + format) and first update scheduled
- [ ] Scope summary sent after kickoff ("here's what we agreed" email — your paper trail)

## Part 4 — Boundary Scripts (copy-paste when you need them)

**Scope creep:**
> "Happy to add that! It sits outside our agreed scope, so I'll send over a quick quote —
> if it's approved by [date] I can fold it in without moving the deadline."

**Late payment (day 3):**
> "Quick nudge — invoice [#] was due on [date]. Could you check where it's got to? Work on
> [next milestone] resumes once it's in."

**The 11pm message:**
> "Saw your message — I'll pick this up first thing tomorrow. For anything urgent outside
> [hours], [emergency option/rate]."

**The disappearing client:**
> "As I've not heard back since [date], I'll pause the project on [date+7] to free the slot.
> Picking it back up after that is a [restart fee / re-booking into the next window]."
