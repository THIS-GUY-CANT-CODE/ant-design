## The 30-Day Launch Plan

### Week 1 — Foundations (evenings, keep the day job)
- **Day 1:** Define the one-line offer: "I help [who] with [what] so they get [outcome]."
  If you can't fill it in, you're selling your CV, not a service — narrow until it fills.
- **Day 2:** Set up Freelancer Finance OS: check the Tax Settings tab, set your set-aside rate.
  You now have a finance system before your first invoice. Most freelancers do this in year two. You're ahead.
- **Day 3:** Price with the floor formula: (target monthly income ÷ realistic billable hours)
  × 1.4 (the 1.4 covers tax, gaps, admin). That's your floor, not your rate.
- **Day 4:** One-page portfolio/proof: three examples of the work (make demo pieces if you
  have no clients yet — a demo made for a real business beats a mock-up).
- **Day 5:** Tell HMRC when required: you must register for Self Assessment once you're
  trading (the £1,000 trading allowance gives casual income some slack — the OS tracks this).
- **Weekend:** Rest. Burnout in week 1 is a rookie move.

### Week 2 — First outreach (Cold Outreach 30 opens here)
- **Day 8:** Build a list of 30 real prospects — businesses you can name a specific
  improvement for. Quality of list > cleverness of email.
- **Day 9–12:** Send 5/day using openers #1–#5. Personalise the [brackets] — that IS the work.
  Log every send in a simple sheet (or the Content Calendar if you have it).
- **Day 13:** First follow-ups (templates #11–#12) to day-8 sends. Most replies live in the follow-up.

### Week 3 — Conversations
- **Day 15+:** Replies get templates #19–#25 (pricing, "send more info", call booking).
- Keep sending 5 fresh ones daily. Pipeline is a habit, not an event.
- **First "yes":** STOP. Open the Client Onboarding Pack. Send Email 1 within 2 hours,
  questionnaire same day, contract + deposit invoice from Freelancer Finance OS's numbering.
  Deposit before work. No exceptions — the pack's boundary scripts exist because everyone
  breaks this rule exactly once.

### Week 4 — Deliver & systemise
- Deliver brilliantly to client #1 (they're your testimonial, referral, and case study).
- Log every payment in the OS; move the set-aside figure to a separate account same day.
- **Day 28:** Send the testimonial ask (#27) and referral ask (#26).
- **Day 30:** Review: sends → replies → calls → clients. Fix the weakest ratio, not the
  loudest anxiety. Repeat week 2–4 until full.

## The honest FAQ

**Can I really get clients in 30 days?** Some do, many take 60–90. The plan's job is to make
sure the *only* variable is volume × time, not "did I do the right things".

**Do I need a limited company?** Not to start. Sole trader is one form; a company is
paperwork you can graduate into. (Not tax advice — ask an accountant when revenue justifies it.)

**What if I hate selling?** Cold Outreach 30 is built for you — every template leads with
usefulness, not pitch. Sending 5 useful emails a day is not "selling", it's being findable.
