# The Etsy Listing SEO Checklist + Fill-In Template

Work through this once per listing. It's the difference between "posted" and "findable".

---

## Part 1 — Before you write anything

- [ ] **Find the real search phrase.** Type your product's obvious name into the Etsy search
  bar and note the autocomplete suggestions — those are real buyer searches. Pick ONE primary
  phrase (2–4 words, e.g. "wedding budget spreadsheet") and 3–5 secondary phrases.
- [ ] **Check the first page for your primary phrase.** If every result has 1,000+ reviews,
  go one niche narrower ("wedding budget spreadsheet UK", "ADHD wedding planner"). You win by
  being specific, not by outranking giants.
- [ ] **Note what page-1 listings show in their thumbnails** — then plan yours to look
  clearly different (different background colour is the cheapest differentiation there is).

## Part 2 — Title (140 chars)

- [ ] Primary phrase is the FIRST thing in the title, word-for-word.
- [ ] Format: `[Primary phrase] | [what it is] | [key benefit or format] | [audience]`
  e.g. `Wedding Budget Spreadsheet UK | Google Sheets & Excel Planner | Track Payments & Cost Per Guest | Instant Download`
- [ ] No keyword soup — it must read like English; Etsy penalises stuffing and buyers bounce.
- [ ] Instant-download products: say "Instant Download" or "Digital Download" — it lifts CTR.

## Part 3 — The 13 tags (use ALL 13)

- [ ] Every tag is a multi-word phrase (single words are wasted slots).
- [ ] Tags 1–5: your primary + secondary phrases exactly.
- [ ] Tags 6–9: audience & occasion angles ("engagement gift idea", "bride organiser").
- [ ] Tags 10–13: format & style angles ("google sheets template", "printable planner").
- [ ] No word repeated across more than ~3 tags (diversify the surface area).
- [ ] Check plurals: Etsy mostly matches them, so don't burn tags on singular+plural pairs.

## Part 4 — Description

- [ ] First 2 lines restate the primary phrase naturally (search engines index them, and
  that's all mobile shows before "more").
- [ ] Then, in order: what they get → who it's for → how it works (3 steps) → what's inside
  (bullet list) → FAQ (3 questions) → refund/licence note.
- [ ] Write sizes/compatibility explicitly ("works with the free Google Sheets app").
- [ ] End with 2–3 sentences of honest scope ("this is an estimator, not tax advice") —
  fewer refunds, better reviews.

## Part 5 — Images (all 10 slots)

1. Hero: product on clean background + 4-word benefit text
2. What's-inside overview (all tabs/pages fanned out)
3–5. Close-ups of the 3 best features (real screenshots, readable text)
6. "How it works" — 3 steps in one graphic
7. Social proof / review quote (once you have one)
8. Compatibility banner (Excel/Sheets/print icons)
9. FAQ graphic
10. Size/format spec card
- [ ] Video slot filled — even a 15-second screen recording of scrolling the file lifts
  conversion measurably.

## Part 6 — After publishing

- [ ] Price ends in .99 or .50; launch 20–30% under your target for the first 10 sales, then raise.
- [ ] Renew or re-photograph the listing if it gets <1 view/day after 30 days (thumbnail is
  usually the culprit, not the SEO).
- [ ] Answer every message within 24h (response time affects search placement).
- [ ] After each sale, use the thank-you note template below.

> **Thank-you note:** "Thanks so much for your order! Any trouble opening the file, message
> me and I'll fix it same-day. If it makes your [planning/budgeting] easier, a review would
> mean the world to a small shop like mine. — [Name]"
