# Project 22 — 20-Product Roadmap

Goal: a catalog of 20 simple, real, sellable digital products. Built with free tools only,
zero-maintenance landing pages, sold via Gumroad (and later Etsy). Research basis: 2026
digital-product market — spreadsheets/templates/planners are the highest-margin, easiest-entry
categories; hyper-specific niches convert better than generic; "done-for-you" bundles trending.
